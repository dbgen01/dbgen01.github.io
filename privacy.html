Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Santvani Bot is used only by the owner of the Santvani channels. It uses the YouTube API Services to upload the owner's own videos, with their titles and descriptions, to the owner's own YouTube channel.

The app does not collect, sell or share personal data of any other person. Access tokens are stored privately as encrypted secrets and are used only to upload the owner's videos. No analytics or advertising data is collected.

By using this app you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page.
